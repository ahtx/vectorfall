# Contributing to VectorFall

Bug reports, questions, test results and ideas are welcome. You do not need to sign a contributor agreement to
open an issue or to use VectorFall.

## Bug reports

Give the information that we need to check the problem:

1. The VectorFall version (`vfctl version`).
2. The card and the driver (`nvidia-smi -L` and the driver version).
3. The output of `vfctl doctor`.
4. The steps that cause the problem, and the log (`vfctl logs`).

## Code and other material

Before we include submitted material, we ask for explicit agreement to [CLA.md](CLA.md). You keep your copyright.
The agreement lets Amir Husain include the work in VectorFall under its public license (now the PolyForm
Noncommercial License 1.0.0) and under a separate commercial license.

AI-assisted work is welcome on the same terms. Tell us the tools and the third-party sources, examine the work, and
confirm that you have the rights to give the licenses. If you are not sure about the rights, tell us before you make
the promise. A signed agreement does not mean that we merge a submission. We also examine scope, correctness and
maintenance.
